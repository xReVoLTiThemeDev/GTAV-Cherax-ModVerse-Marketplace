# ModVerse Vehicles: Attribution correction and removal requests

If you own or created a vehicle save, screenshot, or other asset listed in the ModVerse Vehicles marketplace, you can request a credit correction, source clarification, or removal.

**Contact:** Open a new [GitHub issue in this repository](https://github.com/xReVoLTiThemeDev/GTAV-Cherax-ModVerse-Marketplace/issues) with a clear title such as `Vehicle credit correction` or `Vehicle removal request`. If the details are private or sensitive, do not post private information or permission screenshots publicly; request a private contact method instead.

For faster identification, provide the vehicle title, the catalog ID or original Discord message ID when known, a brief description of the requested change, and enough non-sensitive information for maintainers to confirm the claim.

Maintainers should review claims promptly, stop listing disputed files pending review when appropriate, remove or correct the public catalog entry, and remove public files/thumbnails if publication rights cannot be demonstrated. GitHub's commit history and third-party caches may persist; discuss residual copies openly rather than promising that historical copies have disappeared. No submission fee is required.

This is a community content-management process, not a substitute for GitHub's official copyright infringement reporting procedures.
